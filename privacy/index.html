Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-27

This policy explains what information Used.Market ("we", "us") collects when you use this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep collection to the minimum needed to run the site.

1. Information you give us

  • Forms (Get Offers, contact, partnership, newsletter, contest entries, job applications, pledges): the details you type, such as name, email, phone, location and item information. These are delivered to our inbox through the form-processing service FormSubmit.
  • Get Offers requests: with your consent, we share your request with buyers and resale partners relevant to your category and location so they can contact you with offers. Partners must use your details only to respond to your request.
  • Donations are processed by PayPal. We receive your name, email and the amount; we never see your card or bank details.

2. Information our tools don't collect

The Scam Checker, Value Estimator, Fee Calculator, Listing Builder and other tools run in your browser. What you type into them is not sent to us. Checklist progress and preferences (such as dark mode) are stored only on your device using browser storage.

3. Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings or, for some third-party vendors, at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Our cookie banner lets you choose non-personalised ads. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland may also see Google's certified consent message.

See our Cookie Policy for details.

4. Embedded content and services

  • YouTube videos use privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play; YouTube's privacy policy then applies.
  • Google Fonts are loaded from Google's servers, which receive your IP address.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.
  • The Recall Checker may query the U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission's public database directly from your browser.

5. How we use information

To respond to you, route offer requests you asked for, run contests, process donations and pledges, send the newsletter you signed up for, improve the site, prevent abuse and comply with law. We don't sell personal information.

6. Retention

Form submissions are kept for up to 24 months unless you ask us to delete them sooner, or longer if required by law (for example, contest and donation records).

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under the GDPR (EU/UK), the CCPA/CPRA (California), PIPEDA (Canada) and Quebec's Law 25 — you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your information, to withdraw consent and to object to certain uses. To make a request, use our contact form and choose "Privacy request". You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

8. Children

Used.Market is intended for adults. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or 16 where applicable). Contests are open only to adults.

9. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, so please don't send sensitive data such as ID or bank numbers through our forms.

10. Changes

We'll post any changes on this page with a new "last updated" date.

11. Contact

Questions? Use our contact form.
