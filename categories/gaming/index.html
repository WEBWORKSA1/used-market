Buying used consoles & games: what the pros check

  • See it running a game, not just the menu.
  • Check controllers for stick drift and trigger response.
  • Ask whether the console is account-banned from online play.
  • Listen for loud fans and check for overheating smells.

Must-pass checks before you pay

  • Powers on and runs a game
  • Not banned from online services

Selling used consoles & games for the best price

  • Deregister/deactivate as primary console and factory reset.
  • Bundle controllers and games — bundles sell faster locally.
  • Retro games: photograph labels, manuals and cartridge pins.

Red flags

  • Won't show it running
  • Sealed 'new' console far below retail from a new account

How fast do consoles & games lose value?

Our estimator uses a typical curve for this category: about 25% in year one, then roughly 12% a year, with a floor near 15% of the original price for working items. Brand, condition, completeness (box, accessories, receipts) and seasonal demand move the number up or down.

As a rule of thumb, consoles & games lose about 25% of their value in the first year and around 12% a year after that, depending on brand and condition. Use the Used Value Estimator, then confirm with recent sold listings.
