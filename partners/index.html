Frequently asked questions

Do sponsors affect rankings?

No. Tool results and the directory trust score are calculated from published fees and features. Sponsored placements are labelled 'Sponsored' and sit outside the rankings.

What does a lead partnership cost?

Pricing depends on category and territory — typically a fixed monthly fee or a price per qualified request. Tell us your categories and area and we'll quote.

Is the domain or website for sale?

We're open to conversations about acquisition, investment and joint ventures. Use the form and choose 'Domain / website acquisition'.
