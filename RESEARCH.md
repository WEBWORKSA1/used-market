## What the research says (and how it shaped the build)

1. **Nobody neutral answers "where should I sell this?"** Every platform argues for itself. Price guides (Reverb, KBB, WorthPoint, Sweetwater) and meta-search (Faircado, BookScouter) are vertical. A cross-category, platform-neutral router + fee calculator + safety layer is the open position. Used.Market takes it.
2. **Trust is the product.** Swappa, Vinted, Trade Me, Carousell (MHA rating) and Craigslist all centre safety. Scam Checker, Recall Checker, Meetup Planner and inspection checklists are the highest-intent, highest-return tools.
3. **Free calculators drive traffic** (Crosslist, KBB, Edmunds). Every tool page carries ads, an email capture and a contextual lead CTA.
4. **Proven monetisation mix for a used-goods network:** display ads + featured/sponsored placements (Used.ca, OfferUp) + lead funnels (KBB, Edmunds, Gazelle) + donations/sponsorship (Freecycle, ShopGoodwill).
5. **The market keeps growing:** ThredUp's 2026 report projects a $393B global secondhand apparel market by 2030 — apparel alone, before cars, electronics and furniture.
