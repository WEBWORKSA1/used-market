Luxury resale is a confidence business. The same bag sells for very different prices depending on whether the buyer believes it's real and complete. Authentication and documentation are how you earn that belief.

How luxury platforms authenticate

Large luxury resellers combine trained human authenticators with technology. One describes in-person inspection by specialists, image comparison against a large database, and X-ray fluorescence machines that measure the metal content of hardware and jewelry. Peer-to-peer luxury marketplaces verify listings independently and promise refunds if an item proves not genuine. Fashion marketplaces that authenticate digitally can occasionally flag genuine items — a real trade-off sellers mention.

Documentation that raises the price

  • Original receipt or proof of purchase.
  • Box, dust bag, authenticity cards, care booklets.
  • For watches: papers, warranty card and service history.
  • Clear photos of serials, date codes, stamps, stitching and hardware engravings.

Pricing luxury

Iconic styles in classic colours can hold much of their retail value; seasonal pieces drop faster. Research realised prices for the exact model, size and colour. Our Value Estimator uses a gentle curve and a high floor for luxury, but comparables matter most.

Red flags when buying luxury

  • "Authentic, no receipt, need it gone today" at a steep discount.
  • Refusal to show close-ups of serials or date codes.
  • Stock photos instead of the actual item.
