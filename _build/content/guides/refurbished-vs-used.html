How refurbishment grades work

Refurbished marketplaces grade devices by cosmetic condition — typically from "fair" to "excellent" — while requiring all to work fully. One large refurbished marketplace says its verified devices pass a minimum 25-point inspection by industry professionals. Read each seller's grade definitions; "good" on one site may be "fair" on another.

Which is best value?

  • Private-sale used is cheapest but carries the most risk — use our checklists.
  • Refurbished with a warranty is the sweet spot for phones, tablets and laptops for most people.
  • Open-box makes sense when the discount is meaningful and you want near-new condition.
