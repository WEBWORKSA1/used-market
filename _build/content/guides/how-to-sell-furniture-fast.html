Furniture is the one category where logistics, not price, usually decides whether a sale happens. A great sofa that the buyer can't get home doesn't sell. Solve transport and presentation, and furniture moves fast.

Know what you're selling

Mass-market furniture typically loses most of its value as soon as it leaves the store, while solid wood, designer, mid-century and antique pieces hold — or gain — value. Look for a maker's label under drawers, seats or on the back panel. A brand name in your title can double the number of buyers who find you.

Pick the right channel

  • Local pickup apps — fastest for everyday furniture; no fees, but you handle messages and meetups.
  • Managed furniture marketplaces — list for free, and they schedule professional pickup, inspection and delivery; you give up a commission.
  • Curated vintage and design marketplaces — best prices for designer, antique and mid-century pieces; listings are screened.
  • Consignment shops — hands-off for quality pieces in your city.

The Where-to-Sell Router ranks these for your value and region.

Photos that sell furniture

  • Clear the space and shoot in daylight from slightly above eye level.
  • Front, three-quarter angle, side, back, close-ups of fabric and wood grain, the label, and every flaw.
  • One photo in context (in a room) helps buyers imagine scale.

The listing details buyers need

Exact dimensions (width × depth × height), material, brand and model, age, condition, smoke-free/pet-free status, whether it comes apart, and pickup details (floor, elevator, stairs). The Listing Builder turns these into a clean description.

Solve transport for the buyer

State that you can help carry it to the curb, offer delivery for a fee, or point buyers to on-demand delivery services that pick up marketplace purchases with movers and a truck — some quote an upfront price based on distance and labour. Removing the "how do I get it home?" question is the single best way to sell faster.

Safety for at-home pickups

Move the item to a garage or entryway, have someone with you, keep valuables out of sight and take payment before loading. See the Meetup Planner.
