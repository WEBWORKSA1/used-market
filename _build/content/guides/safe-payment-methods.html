Most marketplace fraud is payment fraud. Choosing the right method — for the value, and for whether you're meeting or shipping — prevents almost all of it.

Why platform checkout wins for shipped items

Protected marketplaces hold the buyer's money until delivery is confirmed and offer refunds for items that don't arrive or aren't as described. One fashion marketplace releases payment to sellers only after the buyer confirms everything is OK, with a short claim window. New Zealand's largest marketplace covers buyers when they pay with its approved methods. The protection only works when you pay through the platform — which is exactly why scammers try to move you off it.

In-person deals

  • As a buyer: inspect first, pay second. Never send a deposit to "hold" an item.
  • As a seller: count cash on the spot; for transfers, wait until the money shows in your banking app. Screenshots and emails prove nothing.
  • For high values, meet at a bank. Tellers can count cash, check notes and process transfers on the spot.

The red-flag phrases

"Refund the difference", "my shipper will collect", "upgrade to a business account to receive", "send me the code", "pay a deposit to hold". If you see one, run the conversation through the Scam Checker and read the 12 common scams.
