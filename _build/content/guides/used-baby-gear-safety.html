Babies outgrow everything in months, which makes second-hand baby gear one of the best deals in the used market. It's also where the safety stakes are highest. The good news: a handful of checks separate the bargains from the risks.

Great value used

  • Clothes, sleep sacks (check sizing and wear), shoes.
  • Books and most toys — check for small parts, loose magnets and button batteries.
  • Strollers and high chairs that aren't recalled and have all parts.
  • Bouncers, play mats, baby carriers in good condition with labels intact.

Buy new (or be very sure)

  • Car seats unless you know the full history: never in a crash, not expired, complete, not recalled.
  • Cribs of unknown age: older designs such as drop-side cribs no longer meet current standards.
  • Mattresses: hygiene and fit matter.
  • Inclined sleepers and products marketed for infant sleep that don't meet current sleep-product standards — regulators have issued repeated warnings and recalls in this category.
  • Breast pumps designed as single-user devices.

The recall check

Recalled baby products don't disappear; they circulate on marketplaces. Recent recall notices for infant products routinely tell consumers to stop using them and not to sell or give them away. Before you buy or sell:

  1. Find the model number and manufacture date on the label.
  2. Search it in our Recall Checker, which refreshes daily from U.S. CPSC data.
  3. For car seats, also check your national vehicle-safety regulator's recall database.
  4. Outside the U.S., check Health Canada, the U.K. product-safety alerts list or the EU Safety Gate.

Inspection essentials

  • All parts, hardware and instructions present — missing crib hardware is a serious hazard.
  • Straps, buckles and brakes work.
  • No cracks, sharp edges, peeling paint or loose parts.
  • Labels present (model, date, standard compliance).

Use the interactive baby gear checklist on your phone at the pickup.

Selling baby gear responsibly

Check recalls before listing, photograph the model and date label, list everything included, and disclose wear. Complete, clean, recall-checked gear sells fast in local parent groups and kids' marketplaces.
