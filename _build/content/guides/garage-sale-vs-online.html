A garage sale turns forty small items into cash in a single morning. Online marketplaces get more per item but cost time per item. The right choice depends on what you have.

The trade-off in one table

Garage saleOnline
Price per itemLowHigher
Time per itemSeconds30–45 minutes
Best forMany low-value itemsFewer, higher-value items
EffortOne intense daySpread over weeks

The hybrid plan

  1. Pull out anything worth more than about $50 and sell it online first — use the Router.
  2. Hold a garage sale for everything else; advertise it on local apps and neighbourhood networks with photos.
  3. Half-price the last two hours.
  4. Give away or donate the remainder the same day.

Garage sale checklist

  • Check local rules or permits.
  • Price everything with stickers; group "everything $1" tables.
  • Have small notes and coins, plus a payment app QR code you control.
  • Keep cash on you, not in an unattended box.
  • Don't let buyers into the house.
  • Don't sell recalled items — especially baby gear. Check the Recall Checker.
