Accessibility Statement

Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

We want everyone to be able to use Used.Market's tools and guides. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we've done

  • Semantic HTML, headings and landmarks; a skip-to-content link on every page.
  • Full keyboard support with visible focus indicators.
  • Labels on every form field and live regions for tool results.
  • Colour contrast checked for text and controls in light and dark themes.
  • Respect for reduced-motion preferences.
  • Videos load only on request and use the standard YouTube player with captions where the creator provides them.

Known limitations

Third-party ads and embedded videos are outside our direct control. Some older embedded videos may lack accurate captions.

Report a barrier

Use our contact form and describe the page and the problem. We aim to respond within five business days.
