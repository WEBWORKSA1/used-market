    How to use a checklist without annoying the seller

    Tell the seller up front that you'll take ten minutes to check the item. Serious sellers expect it — and the ones who refuse are telling you something. Bring what you need (a charger, a SIM card, a light, a tape measure), work through the must-pass checks first, and only then move to condition details.

    Use the condition findings to negotiate politely: "The battery is at 78%, which means a replacement soon — would you take $40 less?" is a fair, specific ask. If a must-pass check fails, don't negotiate. Walk away.
