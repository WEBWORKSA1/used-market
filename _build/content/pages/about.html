About Used.Market

The used economy is enormous and growing — and confusing. Every platform has its own fees, rules and safety gaps, and every one of them tells you it's the best place to sell. We built Used.Market to be the neutral page you check first.

What we do

  • Free tools that answer the practical questions: where to sell, what it's worth, what you'll keep after fees, whether a message is a scam, whether a product was recalled, and what to check before paying.
  • Guides grounded in platform policies, regulator guidance and real sold data — with sources linked.
  • A neutral directory of 50+ marketplaces, buyback services and consignment sites, with a transparent trust score.
  • Offers on request: if you'd rather not sell it yourself, we route your request to relevant buyers and resale partners.

What we don't do

We are not a marketplace. We don't list items, hold money, ship goods or take a cut of your sale. We don't sell rankings: tool results come from published fees and features.

How we're funded

Advertising (including Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, referral fees from partners who receive offer requests, and reader support. Details are in our advertising disclosure.

How we research

Before launch we opened and studied 39 leading marketplaces, buyback services, price guides and consumer-safety regulators across North America, Europe, Asia and Oceania, and recorded what each actually does. Fees are re-checked regularly and dated; recalls and news refresh automatically every day. Read our editorial policy.
