How the Router decides

Most "where to sell" advice is written by a platform about itself. The Router is neutral: it scores every platform in our directory that serves your category and region on four things that actually determine your outcome.

  • Speed — how quickly typical listings in that category sell or how fast an instant offer pays.
  • Price realised — whether the audience pays close to full market value or expects a bargain.
  • Effort — photos, messages, meetups and shipping versus "they handle everything".
  • Trust — on-platform payment, identity checks, buyer/seller protection and transaction records.

Your priority changes how those four are weighted. Then a few common-sense rules kick in: below about $30, local pickup beats shipping because postage eats the margin; above $1,000, platforms without protected payment drop; designer goods favour places that authenticate; bulky furniture favours local or managed delivery.

Read the payout column correctly

For marketplaces with published formulas, we subtract seller and processing fees from your value. For instant buyback and car-offer services we show the midpoint of a typical offer range, and for consignment the midpoint of a typical consignor share. Treat those as starting points, then get real quotes.
