The principles behind the plan

Every serious marketplace safety guide boils down to the same few rules: deal locally and face-to-face where you can, keep conversations and payments on the platform, never pay or accept payment before meeting unless the platform protects it, and meet somewhere public. The planner turns those rules into a concrete plan that scales with the money at stake.

Why the payment advice changes with value

Under a couple of hundred dollars, counterfeit-cash risk is small and convenience wins. As value rises, so does the incentive to fake payments — so the plan moves you toward methods you can verify yourself, in your own banking app or at a bank counter, before anything changes hands.
