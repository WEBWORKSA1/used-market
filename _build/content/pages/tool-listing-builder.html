Why structured listings sell faster

Marketplace search engines match the words buyers type. Buyers type brand, model and the spec they filter by — not adjectives. A title like "Herman Miller Aeron Chair Size B Graphite Excellent" matches far more searches than "Amazing office chair!!!". The description then answers the questions buyers would otherwise message you about: condition, what's included, flaws and logistics.

Honesty pays twice. Clear disclosure of flaws cuts "not as described" disputes — which on protected marketplaces can mean a refund and a return at your cost — and earns the reviews that make your next sale easier.
