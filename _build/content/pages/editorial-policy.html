Editorial Policy & Methodology

Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

Independence

Editorial decisions — what we cover, how tools rank platforms, what we recommend — are made independently of advertisers, sponsors and partners. No one can pay to change a tool result, a trust score or a guide's conclusions.

Sources

We rely on primary sources wherever possible: platforms' own fee and policy pages, regulators (for example consumer-product-safety and tax authorities), and published industry research. We link sources in each guide. Secondary sources are used to cross-check, not to replace primary ones.

Fees and dates

Every fee in the calculator and directory carries the date it was last checked. Fees marked "approx." have published structures that vary by country, category or plan. When we learn of a change, we update the data file and the date.

How tools work

  • Trust score (0–100): on-platform payment (40), identity verification (30), buyer/seller protection (20), transaction records (10). These criteria mirror the safeguards government marketplace-safety ratings assess.
  • Where-to-Sell Router: weighted scores for speed, price realised, effort and trust, adjusted by transparent rules described on the tool page.
  • Value Estimator: category depreciation curves with published multipliers for condition, brand, demand and completeness.
  • Scam Checker: pattern rules drawn from marketplace safety centres and consumer-protection guidance.

Automation

Recall data and news headlines refresh automatically each day. Automated sections are labelled. News items are headlines with links; the stories belong to their publishers.

Corrections

Spot an error? Tell us. We fix factual errors promptly and update the page date.

Advertising and partners

Ads are served by Google AdSense and labelled "Advertisement". Sponsored placements are labelled "Sponsored". See our advertising disclosure.
