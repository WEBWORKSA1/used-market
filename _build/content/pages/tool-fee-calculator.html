How each platform charges

Marketplace fees come in three shapes, and knowing which one you're dealing with matters more than the headline percentage.

  • Seller commission — a percentage of the sale (sometimes including shipping) plus a small fixed fee. eBay, Mercari, Poshmark, Etsy, Grailed, Whatnot and Reverb work this way.
  • Processing only — no commission, just card processing. Depop's U.S. and U.K. model is the best-known example.
  • Buyer pays — sellers keep 100% and the buyer pays a protection fee at checkout, as on Vinted. Your price has to absorb that in buyers' minds.

Cheapest isn't always best

A platform that charges 20% but sells your jacket in two days at full price can beat a 0% app where it sits for two months and sells for 40% less. Pair this calculator with the Where-to-Sell Router, which weighs speed and audience as well as fees.

Before you rely on a number

Fees change, vary by category and country, and some platforms add fees for promoted listings, currency conversion or instant payouts. We date every figure; confirm on the platform's fee page before you list.
