Used.Market is free to use. Here is exactly how it's funded.

  • Display advertising from Google AdSense, labelled "Advertisement". We don't choose individual ads.
  • Sponsorships of categories, tools or newsletter issues, always labelled "Sponsored" or "Presented by".
  • Referral fees: partners who receive offer requests you submit through Get Offers may pay us a fee. Some outbound links may in future become affiliate links; if so, they will be marked.
  • Reader support: donations and pledges.

What money can't buy

Tool rankings, trust scores, fee data and guide conclusions are never for sale and are calculated the same way for every platform, paying or not.

This disclosure is provided in the spirit of advertising-disclosure rules such as the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's Endorsement Guides and similar rules elsewhere.
