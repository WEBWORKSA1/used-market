What the checker looks for

Marketplace scams are repetitive. The same scripts show up on every platform, in every country, because they work. Safety teams and regulators — from the classifieds sites' own help centres to national consumer agencies — describe the same handful of patterns. The checker tests your message against 24 of them, weighted by how strongly each predicts fraud:

  • Irreversible payment: gift cards, wires, crypto and money orders.
  • Fake money: cashier's cheques, overpayments and "refund the difference".
  • Account takeover: requests for verification codes, card details or bank logins.
  • Fake confirmations: emails claiming payment is "pending" until you upgrade an account or pay a fee.
  • Distance: overseas, deployed or "my shipper will collect", and refusals to inspect.
  • Pressure: urgency, deposits to hold, links and QR codes.

The one rule that stops most scams

Keep the conversation and the payment inside the platform — or meet in person, inspect, and pay or get paid on the spot. Almost every scam tries to break one of those two things.
