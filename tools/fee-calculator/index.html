How each platform charges

Marketplace fees come in three shapes, and knowing which one you're dealing with matters more than the headline percentage.

  • Seller commission — a percentage of the sale (sometimes including shipping) plus a small fixed fee. eBay, Mercari, Poshmark, Etsy, Grailed, Whatnot and Reverb work this way.
  • Processing only — no commission, just card processing. Depop's U.S. and U.K. model is the best-known example.
  • Buyer pays — sellers keep 100% and the buyer pays a protection fee at checkout, as on Vinted. Your price has to absorb that in buyers' minds.

Cheapest isn't always best

A platform that charges 20% but sells your jacket in two days at full price can beat a 0% app where it sits for two months and sells for 40% less. Pair this calculator with the Where-to-Sell Router, which weighs speed and audience as well as fees.

Before you rely on a number

Fees change, vary by category and country, and some platforms add fees for promoted listings, currency conversion or instant payouts. We date every figure; confirm on the platform's fee page before you list.

Frequently asked questions

Which selling platform has the lowest fees?

For sellers, Vinted (buyer pays the protection fee) and local pickup on Facebook Marketplace, OfferUp, Kijiji or Craigslist charge nothing. Among shipped marketplaces, Depop's US/UK model (no commission, payment processing only) is among the cheapest. Poshmark's 20% is the highest of the big fashion apps.

Do platforms charge fees on shipping?

Some do. eBay, Mercari, Etsy, Grailed, Reverb and Facebook's shipped orders calculate their percentage on the total including shipping charged to the buyer. The calculator applies that automatically.

Why is my eBay fee different?

eBay's final value fee varies by category and seller level, and store subscriptions lower it. We use the most common rate (about 13.6% plus $0.40 per order). Enter your own rate in the custom row to model your exact case.

How current are these fees?

Each platform shows when its fee was checked. Fees marked 'approx.' have published structures that vary by country or plan. Always confirm on the platform's own fee page before relying on a number.
