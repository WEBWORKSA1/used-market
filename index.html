Frequently asked questions

What is Used.Market?

Used.Market is an independent, free information service for anyone buying or selling used goods. We don't list items or take a cut of sales; we help you decide where to sell, what to charge, what each platform takes, and how to avoid scams and recalled products.

Is Used.Market a marketplace?

No. We don't buy, sell, hold or ship items. When you request offers, we route your request to buyers and resale partners, and you deal with them directly.

How do you make money?

Through advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships and partner referrals, and reader support. Rankings in our tools are calculated from published fees and features, not from payments. See our advertising disclosure.

Are the fee numbers accurate?

They are checked against public fee breakdowns and dated on the calculator. Platforms change fees often, so confirm on the platform before you list.

Is the Scam Checker private?

Yes. The checker runs in your browser and the message you paste is not sent to us or anyone else.
