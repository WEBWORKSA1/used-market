Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Requesting offers costs nothing and you're never obliged to accept. Partners who receive qualified requests may pay us a referral fee, which never changes what you're offered.

Who will contact me?

Independent buyers and resale partners suited to your category and location — for example device buyback services, consignment shops, estate-sale companies, furniture resellers or car buyers. We only pass your details to partners relevant to your request.

How fast will I hear back?

Most requests get a first response within one to two business days. Urgent or high-value requests are prioritised.

Do you buy items yourselves?

No. Used.Market doesn't buy, sell or hold items. We match your request with partners and you deal with them directly.

What if I'd rather sell it myself?

Use the free Where-to-Sell Router and Fee Calculator to pick the best platform and price.
