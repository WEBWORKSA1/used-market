Do I Pay Tax When I Sell My Used Stuff? 1099-K Rules for 2026

When selling personal items is taxable, how the 2026 Form 1099-K threshold ($20,000 and 200 transactions) works, and what flippers should track.

By the Used.Market editorial teamUpdated 2026-11-277 min read

In this guide

  1. The short answer
  2. The 2026 Form 1099-K threshold
  3. Getting a 1099-K doesn't mean you owe tax
  4. Not getting a 1099-K doesn't mean you don't owe tax
  5. A simple record-keeping system

General information, not tax advice

This guide summarises U.S. federal rules as of the date above. State rules and other countries differ. Speak to a tax professional about your situation.

The short answer

If you're decluttering — selling your own used things for less than you paid — you generally don't owe U.S. federal income tax on those sales, because there's no gain. If you sell something for more than you paid, or you buy items to resell, the profit is taxable income.

The 2026 Form 1099-K threshold

Form 1099-K is the information return that payment apps and online marketplaces send to sellers and to the IRS. After several years of changes, the rules settled:

Tax yearPlatforms must issue a 1099-K when…
2023 and earlierMore than $20,000 and more than 200 transactions
2024More than $5,000 (no transaction minimum)
2025More than $20,000 and more than 200 transactions
2026More than $20,000 and more than 200 transactions

The One Big Beautiful Bill Act, signed in July 2025, reverted the threshold to more than $20,000 and more than 200 transactions, and the IRS published FAQs confirming it in October 2025. Both conditions must be met for a platform to be required to send the form.

Getting a 1099-K doesn't mean you owe tax

The form reports gross payments, not profit. If you receive one for personal items sold at a loss, you report the amounts and show that there was no gain. Keep receipts, bank statements or old order confirmations showing what you paid.

Not getting a 1099-K doesn't mean you don't owe tax

Below the threshold, platforms may not send a form, but taxable profit is still taxable. Flippers and resellers should treat it as a small business: track purchase cost, fees, shipping and supplies for every item.

A simple record-keeping system

  • A spreadsheet with: item, date bought, cost, date sold, platform, sale price, fees, shipping, net.
  • Photos of receipts (or screenshots of original orders).
  • Download platform sales reports every quarter.

The Fee Calculator shows profit after fees for each sale, which makes the spreadsheet easy.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 1099-K threshold for 2026?

For 2026, payment platforms and marketplaces generally must issue Form 1099-K when a seller receives more than $20,000 in gross payments and has more than 200 transactions in the calendar year. The One Big Beautiful Bill Act restored this threshold.

Do I owe tax on items I sold at a loss?

Generally, selling personal items for less than you paid is a non-deductible personal loss and isn't taxable income — but keep records showing what you paid. This is general information; check with a tax professional.

Do flippers pay tax?

Yes. Buying items to resell for profit is business activity, and profits are taxable whether or not you receive a 1099-K.
