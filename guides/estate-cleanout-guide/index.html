Estate Cleanouts & Downsizing: How to Sell a Whole Home of Stuff

A calm, practical plan for downsizing or settling an estate: triage, valuation, what to sell yourself, estate-sale companies, consignment, donation and removal.

By the Used.Market editorial teamUpdated 2026-11-278 min read

In this guide

  1. Step 1: Triage into four piles
  2. Step 2: Identify the valuables
  3. Step 3: Choose your channel by pile
  4. Step 4: Do the time maths
  5. Step 5: Get bulk offers

Clearing a whole home — whether downsizing or after a loss — is emotional and exhausting. A clear plan reduces both. The goal isn't to get top dollar for every spoon; it's to find the few items worth real money, move everything else efficiently, and protect your time.

Step 1: Triage into four piles

  • Keep — family items and anything you'd regret.
  • Valuable — jewellery, art, watches, designer goods, quality furniture, collectibles, instruments, tools.
  • Sellable — everyday furniture, appliances, electronics, kitchenware.
  • Donate / recycle / dispose — the rest.

Step 2: Identify the valuables

Look for maker's marks, signatures, hallmarks and labels. Research realized auction prices, not asking prices — collectibles databases hold years of sold results. For jewellery, fine art and anything that looks rare, pay for a professional appraisal before you sell.

Step 3: Choose your channel by pile

PileBest channel
ValuableSpecialist auction, authenticated or curated marketplace, consignment
Sellable furniture & appliancesLocal pickup apps, managed furniture marketplaces
Everything at onceEstate-sale company or bulk buyer
LeftoversCharity donation, free-gifting networks, removal service

Step 4: Do the time maths

Selling forty items yourself can take dozens of hours. List the items in the Declutter Cash Calculator to see your effective hourly rate — then decide what to sell yourself and what to hand to a professional.

Step 5: Get bulk offers

Estate-sale companies, consignment shops and bulk buyers can make a single offer, sometimes including removal. Request estate and bulk offers and compare them with your DIY estimate.

Protect personal information

Wipe computers and phones, shred documents, and remove photos and mail before buyers or crews enter the home.

Frequently asked questions

Should I hire an estate-sale company?

For a full household with furniture, collectibles and everyday goods, often yes. They handle pricing, staffing and sale days for a commission. Get at least two quotes and check references.

How do I find valuable items?

Look for maker's marks, signatures and labels; research realized prices for antiques and collectibles; and get a professional appraisal for jewellery, art and anything that looks rare.
