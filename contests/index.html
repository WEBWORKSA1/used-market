  1. No purchase necessary. A purchase or donation does not increase your chances of winning.
  2. Eligibility: open to individuals 18+ (or the age of majority where they live) in jurisdictions where the contest is lawful. Void where prohibited. Employees of Used.Market and their immediate families are not eligible. Residents of Quebec may need to observe additional local requirements; we will advise winners where applicable.
  3. How to enter: submit the entry form during the entry period. One entry per person per monthly challenge.
  4. Judging: entries are judged on usefulness to other buyers and sellers (40%), originality (30%) and clarity (30%). Judges' decisions are final.
  5. Skill-testing question: where required by law (including in Canada), a potential winner must correctly answer a time-limited mathematical skill-testing question before being confirmed.

  10. Used.Market may modify or cancel a challenge if it cannot run as planned, and will post any change on this page.
