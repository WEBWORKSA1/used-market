Frequently asked questions

Is my donation tax-deductible?

No. Used.Market is an independent publisher, not a registered charity, so donations aren't tax-deductible.

How is the money used?

Operations (hosting, data, tools), promotion and marketing, hiring writers, researchers and video creators, and funding community contest prizes. We publish a short annual summary.

Can I cancel a monthly donation?

Yes, anytime, from your payment account. Monthly support is processed by the payment provider; we never see your card details.

Can I support without money?

Absolutely: share a tool with a friend, report an outdated fee, or enter a contest. You can also pledge support below.
